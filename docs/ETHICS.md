# Ethics and responsible publication

Informal-settlement maps concern communities that may face tenure insecurity, eviction risk, stigma, or unequal service access. A technically valid polygon is not automatically appropriate to publish.

Before publishing a layer:

- Confirm that the source is already public or that publication is authorized.
- Do not include household-level personal information or identifiable resident records.
- Preserve the source's terminology and avoid treating “slum”, “informal settlement”, “deprived area”, “urban village”, and “low-income neighborhood” as interchangeable.
- Record uncertainty, date, method, and known omissions.
- Avoid deriving an “informal” label solely from building density, roof material, street pattern, income proxies, or satellite morphology without validated methodology.
- Prefer community-validated and locally governed data where available.
- Consider whether precise boundaries could create harm; if so, publish a coarser representation or omit the layer.
